**Task 1: A typical lesson about a global, ongoing, important issue.** 
a) Use [**this link**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m1BLsySgsHM) and your mobile phones to watch the content. 
b) You are on the same journey and you can take one bag with you – what belongings will you take? No more than 10. Be ready to justify your answers.
c) Come up with 5 questions of your own to better understand the picture below. [**Use the question grid.**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AMyn8CB--rdeLaBwn1TW7TXDWHmqOjNR0MBSkelX0fg/edit?usp=sharing)

**Task 2:** [**Card Sort Activity**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PMncZ6zpZQIRDdMmrg4FZlhOYVN_G1Np/view?usp=sharing)
a) Organize the cards into groups/ categories you feel are appropriate.
b) What were the groups/ categories you picked? Why? 
c) Using the information you have learned, through discussion with a partner work out an explanation to what you can see in the picture.
d) Write it down independently. 
e) Now go over your explanation and try to use these word:
Forced Migration
Refugee
Migrant
Emigrant
Push factor & pull factor
IDP (internally displaced people) **Extension:**[**Extra propaganda if needed.**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZQrSGU7A2kPbsOEhlzCk44ucc9UPXL1F/view?usp=sharing)
