What is meant by “overshoot day”? 
Why does overshoot day vary on a national scale? Think of several reasons, quoting examples of countries.
Explain the trend in the dates for overshoot day, including anomalies.
Explain what is causing the world to overshoot, and what the consequences are.

Watch this YouTube video and explain the concept of the "ecological footprint".

**Activities:** 
1. 
a) Describe the changes to the world's ecological footprint [**using this**](https://econation.co.nz/ecological-footprint/) (reference the graph's key & quantify your answers). 
b) Suggest how and why ecological footprints might vary according to levels of development. Comment on LICs, MICs and HICs.
c) Suggest reasons for the differences you identify (use **SEEP** in your explanations). 
d) [**Comment on your home country**](assets/EcologicalFootprintMap.jpg). Are there any surprises?

2. Use this [**website**](https://www.footprintcalculator.org/) to work out your own ecological footprint using your school email. 
a) If everyone lived like you how many earths would we need?
b) What date is your individual overshoot day? 
c) Re take the "test" and make changes to your lifestyle during the pandemic. Explain the three things you changed about your lifestyle during the pandemic and why you have become more sustainable.
