**Party people:** 
You have been asked to host a party for the greatest population thinkers of our time (past and present). The problem is they are a boring bunch! You will need to be careful where they are seated so the party-poopers don't spoil the fun.

**Task:**[**In groups**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1u33ju2BlY8OqNxtVQ3Yh3fj-PMlPDMFc2_fF5tEwfzI/edit?usp=sharing)
Find out about who you are as a guest at the party to discuss issues regarding population growth versus resource consumption. 
Create a slide to represent your views as a guest and [**add it here**](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1WDuiH_QXIIlgBP724X55mJn373ST_Y8tIGMEtw09AVs/edit?usp=sharing) (max. of one slide per group) using [**these resources**](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/0B1D3UOqNjNuubFI1VHd3cmRvOXc?resourcekey=0-kPqgRcDDEk3_pDJrABLkxw&usp=sharing)**.**
Discuss/argue with the other guests!

**Debrief:** 
What are the "divergent ways of thinking"? 
Compare and contrast Neo-Malthusian and Anti-Malthusian views on population and resources.

**Plenary:** 
Which of the views do you personally agree with? 
Are you an optimist or a pessimist? 
Can you use an example to support your views?
