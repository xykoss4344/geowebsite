**Starter:** The Story of a Spoon.

**Market Place:** In [**groups**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13QHYOA9EsY0mHjkjWMnjqgRmicNmCI7UaaSyywD21OY/edit?usp=sharing) research and create a poster on a particular form of alternative energy.

You will have 40 minutes to research and create your poster.  You need to use that information to produce a poster containing **no more than 10 words** (drawings, diagrams, hand drawn maps, other visuals are OK). You will use this poster to teach others.

**You must include:** 
How your alternative energy works (the production and mechanisms) - add images,
Locational map to show where this type of energy is prevalent (clearly state whether these places are LICs, MICs or HICs), 
Information related to how this energy is used,
Advantages,
Disadvantages/ criticism of the energy type &
Evaluation of suitability for future mass use. 
After which 'marketplace' will take place in 3 minute rounds where you absorb and summarize the main ideas of other groups or teach. **Important Notice:** There will be a formative quiz after this activity where your scores will be logged.

**Plenary:** 3, 2, 1.

**Homework**: Watch **Cowspiracy** — your teacher will tell you where to find it.
