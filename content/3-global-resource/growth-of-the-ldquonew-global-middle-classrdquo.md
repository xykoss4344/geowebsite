**Activities:** 
a) What do you think of the quote below and the infographic opposite? Why might H.I.C.s have seen a decline in the middle classes?

**"For the first time, a truly global middle class is emerging. By 2030, it will more than double in size, from 2 billion today to 4.9 billion. It is estimated that the European and American middle classes will shrink from 50% of the total to just 22%. Rapid growth in China, India, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia will cause Asia’s share of the new middle to more than double from its current 30%. By 2030, Asia will host 64% of the global middle class and account for over 40% of global middle-class consumption".** **David Rohde, Reuters.**
