**"Global Dimming" (natural causes)**

The eruption of Mount Pinatubo, The Philippines in June 1991 remains the best example of how a volcanic eruption can change the global energy balance and actually cool the planet.

Take some notes from [**this short presentation**](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1SANe0sHM3jL1B_Z2vJ7Hs-Rj2K9yUDhoEfmp2KVswZk/edit?usp=sharing)to explain how the eruption led to climate change via "global dimming".

Afterwards, watch [**this video clip**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EE25MCd86bLWcY8dJLJkUJjwuP3EXGt8/view?usp=sharing) of the eruption and its aftermath.
