**[Add these terms to your glossary:](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_aAe4tLUpYlEylL19cT2MbZasLMEw0_zqx_gFYKKd4c/edit?usp=sharing)**

[Adaptation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb7iW_6b1NI)--a response that seeks to reduce the vulnerability (and therefore increase resilience) of the EFFECTS of climate change. 

[Mitigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change_mitigation)-- actions to limit the magnitude or rate of climate change by focusing on its CAUSES. Climate change mitigation generally involves reductions in human emissions of greenhouse gases (GHGs). 

[Geoengineering](http://www.geoengineeringwatch.org/geoengineering-definition-of-terms/)-- technological methods to **mitigate** the impacts of climate change through artificial modification of the Earth's climate systems on a large scale. 

[Geopolitical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geopolitics)--the political relationships between different places.
