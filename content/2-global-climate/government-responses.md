[**Add these terms to your glossary:**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb7iW_6b1NI)[Adaptation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb7iW_6b1NI)--a response to climate change, that seeks to reduce the vulnerability (and therefore increase resilience) of social and biological systems to relatively sudden change. These actions focus on the EFFECTS of climate change.

[Mitigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change_mitigation)--consists of actions to limit the magnitude or rate of climate change.  Climate change mitigation generally involves reductions in human emissions of greenhouse gases (GHGs).  Mitigation may also be achieved by increasing the capacity of carbon sinks, e.g., through reforestation. These actions therefore focus on the CAUSES of climate change.

[Geo-Engineering](http://www.geoengineeringwatch.org/geoengineering-definition-of-terms/)-- **aims to mitigate the impacts of climate change through** artificial modification of the Earth's climate systems. Two main methods: Solar Radiation management (SRM) and Carbon Dioxide removal (CDR)

[Carbon Offsetting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_offset)-- a mitigation method to reduce emissions of carbon dioxide in order to compensate for or to offset an emission made elsewhere.

[Geopolitical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geopolitics)--the political relationships between different places.
