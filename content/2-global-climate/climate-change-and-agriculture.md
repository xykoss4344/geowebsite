**How climate change affects farmers (and threatens food security) in poorer places**
Read [**this article**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zmfRZ7YfBrBiPw0iuPkNI54McUHk9fdM/view?usp=sharing) to explain why the poorest farmers suffer most. 
**Desertification** is a growing environmental problem in **semi-arid** areas like the **Sahel**, a belt of land across Africa south of the Sahara. Watch [**this video**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wcCq5llmk0AwQ7Kx-wEX7RCCK-T-KmVH/view?usp=sharing) about one country affected, **Burkina Faso**, and how desertification is linked to both climate change and farming practices.

**How climate change affects farmers in richer places (like the USA)**
Use [**this article**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1u2jcnf5SpVw9yUrCzRAih3WhimGiDqBC/view?usp=sharing) to explain the uneven consequences of climate change on agriculture across the USA

**Why soil erosion matters for climate change**
Watch [**this video**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wpBzwPk-lvh2y5RHbNe5rpgxleFvLtXr/view?usp=sharing) and record why soil is so important in terms of climate change thanks to its role in the carbon cycle as the largest terrestrial **carbon sink**. 
Use [**this video**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/13WCFXeMuvJTNNWSYc2DcnWUK4ZMnF5xE/view?usp=sharing) to explain why soil degradation and erosion is such a big issue today, and therefore indirectly linked to climate change as a result.
