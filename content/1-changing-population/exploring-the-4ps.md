**Task 1**: Introducing the 4Ps in the media. 
[**Read your news article and then complete the fact file.**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13D3I3jtxyQm7fWVMsJhRNSRWuxzWLst2B7KE5Smr3kI/edit?usp=sharing)

**Task 2**: [**Use this document.**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kGUvo4a393sr_ef9Eg0EGQpbPQpUSHy0kX_KYK8Mqdw/edit?usp=sharing) 
a) Complete the gap fill electronically 
b) Use the hard copies to complete the picture activity.

Extension - Each group should now be charged with finding two images, ideally contrasting ones within a country or at different levels of economic development. You must study the images carefully and make notes privately on what you see including what the actual place is and what the processes, power and possibilities are.  However, unlike the previous activity, do not add any arrows. Print the sheets out in color and pass them to another group to complete the analysis. Each group should aim to add at least 8 evidence arrows and complete the 4P's grid on the back of each photo.
