**A group challenge …**
Play the role of journalists to gather specific information on the Syria crisis as a case study of forced migration. \

**[Using this folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1MYd13l0dg0uRbTPDCW0b5LXHsBM0R894?usp=sharing)** - collaborate in groups of no more than three to investigate and record:
Why have people been forced to leave Syria (or been internally displaced) in such high numbers? Consider both environmental and political 'push factors". 
What has been the impact on Syria, the country of origin? This should include the plight of the people who have returned to Syria since the end of the civil war in December 2024. 
What has been the impact on Syria’s neighbouring countries and on countries in Europe?
