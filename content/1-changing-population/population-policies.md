**Investigation 1: Anti-natalist policies: Focus on China**
Collaborating in groups, investigate the causes and impacts of China's anti-natalist policy. The One-Child Policy began in 1979 and was replaced by a 'two-child' policy in 2016. 
To shape your investigation, you will use the Six Thinking Hats to evaluate this anti-natalist policy. 

[Here is an introduction](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1SQmNdenxWkCxzviQ0u-1XzXrYl6nz6yhWChhm272zpY/edit?usp=sharing) to get you started. The Six Thinking Hats will be printed for you, along with [three inquiry questions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1LhWtC_sbu1owcEz83FTI0E-XRgWtr7SD/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=101400556587948969931&rtpof=true&sd=true) for each 'hat'. 
Use the resources **[in this folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1LW3oqG6ARYBKxr4dj_Dz3BFIDgkT_3-R?usp=sharing)**, and of course, feel free to find your own, too!

**Investigation 2: Pro-Natalist Policies: Focus on Japan**
For Japan, declining fertility and an ageing population are a national emergency. \
[Here is an introductory presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1FvBu0B4-F70K9ywRf-bR622yu5nLvRYx2QmGL3qVNyc/edit?usp=sharing) to tune you in to the main issues. 

The government has asked consultancy companies to **evaluate** their current policies for both. You represent one of them.

**Consider:** 

* The economic or social benefits.
* The different groups of people are affected both positively and negatively.
* The costs of the policy, including those in the long term. 
* Japan’s cultural preferences for family and cultural preservation. 
* The anticipated response from the Japanese electorate.  

***You are competing with other companies … will you win the Japanese government contract?***  \
To guide you through this activity and help you decide on the most effective policies, you will diamond-rank (and later justify) policies for managing the ageing population and boosting the fertility rate (nine for each). 

[Here are the instructions](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1u8pjcplSAKes_LQhPQKiGMfNp9_XsDZA/view?usp=sharing), including the different policy options.
