**Investigation 1: Anti-natalist policies: Focus on China**
Collaborating in groups, investigate the causes and impacts of China's anti-natalist policies. The One Child policy began in 1979 and changed to a 'two child' policy in 2016. 
To shape your investigation, use [**the six thinking hats**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EVCb0IocisC93GwpxZ5Hb0co_i1atTKW/view?usp=sharing)**.** 
Use the resources [**in this folder**](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1LW3oqG6ARYBKxr4dj_Dz3BFIDgkT_3-R?usp=sharing), and of course feel free to find your own, too!

**Investigation 2: Pro-Natalist Policies**
Start with this overview ([**Guardian video, November 2024**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11zXf4OPU69zTr3H2VCPgS4SRwWWzSlf3/view?usp=sharing)) on why pro-natalist policies are a hot topic politically today, especially among politicians/commentators on the right. According to the latter, why is pro-natalism in many places necessary economically and politically? Note down a few examples that are memorable to you.

**Research to create a case study of how Japan is managing its ageing/potentially declining population.****Make sure you cover:**
The implications of the population situation today (only the **most important** pros and cons)—as you explored earlier
Current and future plans for managing the population - pro-natalist and managing the elderly
A critique of these policies.  **NB:** As with all case studies you will need illustrative material/facts & figures to demonstrate your understanding.
