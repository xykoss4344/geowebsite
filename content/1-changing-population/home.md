**GEOGRAPHY at ISHCMC**

The Diploma Programme geography course integrates both physical and human geography, and ensures that students acquire elements of both scientific and socio-economic methodologies.

The course examines key global issues, such as poverty, sustainability and climate change. It considers examples and detailed case studies at a variety of scales, from local to regional, national and international.

This site has been created for the students at ISHCMC in grades 11 and 12. Thanks to the contemporary nature of the course, it is updated almost every week!
