**Population pyramids**, also called age-gender pyramids, are graphical illustrations that show the distribution of age groups in a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population) at any particular time.

**How to read and interpret population pyramids**

Start by watching this mini TED talk, [Population Pyramids, Powerful Predictors of the Future](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1rkjXRwVvat22wiASd9RQcFZ3KpSOJJ02/view?usp=sharing). 

If you want more help, step-by-step instructions on how to read a population pyramid, look at **[this presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1AWOCPOL8lR8R6t3r5a0qHrc9xOLhaqqf2KbhAVxNC3g/edit?usp=sharing).**

**Your challenge:**

You will use [this website](https://www.populationpyramid.net/world/2026/) and focus on either Vietnam, Kenya or Japan. \
You will: \
1. **Identify** and **interpret** the changes in the population composition from 1950 to 2025. \
2. **Analyse** the changes from 1950 to 2025. \
3. **Evaluate** the changes the country might have to manage by 2050.

When you start looking at the changes in population, the concept of **population momentum** is important to understand. \
**Think -** How is it possible for a country's birth rate to decline, but the population to continue to increase?
