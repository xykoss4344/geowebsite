**Population pyramids**, also called age-gender pyramids, are graphical illustrations that show the distribution of age groups in a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population) at any particular time.

Start by watching this short video, [**"from a pyramid to a spinning top"**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cdIMWBwwwO2qwZ6uCJFeosvrHp8Bo-Tf/view?usp=sharing) to understand how population pyramids work.

**How to read and interpret population pyramids**

a) Take a look at this graphic as an overview. If you want more help step-by-step, look at [**this presentation**](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1AWOCPOL8lR8R6t3r5a0qHrc9xOLhaqqf2KbhAVxNC3g/edit?usp=sharing)**.**

b) Pick a country and use [**this site**](https://www.populationpyramid.net/) to see how its population pyramid has changed over time, and projections into the future.
