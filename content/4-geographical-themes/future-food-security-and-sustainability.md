**Plenary**: **TOK:** Artificial meat stops cruelty to animals, is better for the environment, and it could be safer and more efficient, and even healthier. Animal welfare groups are generally in favor of the production of in vitro meat because it does not have a nervous system and therefore cannot feel pain.

**Q:** Is in vitro meat less objectionable than traditionally obtained meat - as it does not involve killing and reduces the risk of animal cruelty - or is it unnatural and are scientists interfering with nature?
