**Pre-event management strategies** - mitigate against the hazard risk
Unlike adaptation, where people accept hazard risk and adjust accordingly, pre-event management involves new possibilities in technology and engineering/building design that mitigate the hazard risk. Remember adaptation versus mitigation strategies in response to climate change! 
Make a copy of [**this document**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vvuv2z-Ej8ykwlRe6tx4KDBYrwT8lfe7iCR8CmZPkLM/edit?usp=sharing). Use the links to explain the different strategies used in different places.
