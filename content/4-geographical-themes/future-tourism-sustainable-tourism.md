**Homework practice: Plan a long response** - "Using examples from Low Income countries, evaluate whether sustainable tourism is an effective strategy". 
One page plan or two pages in full. 
Intro – define key terms, introduce the main ideas, including definitions of sustainable tourism and ecotourism. 
Main body paragraphs – divide up into benefits/ costs for the environment and people. Use case studies and specific examples from Kenya. Think about people (especially the Maasai) and the environment (especially game parks). 
Conclusion – on balance, is it an effective strategy?
