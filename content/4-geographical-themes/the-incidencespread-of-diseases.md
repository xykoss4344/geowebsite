**Focus on cholera**
Cholera is a disease of the small intestine caused by the bacterium Vibrio Cholerae. It is a water-borne disease although it can also be transmitted by contaminated food. For cholera outbreaks to occur, there must be two conditions: Significant breaches of water sanitation leading to contamination by the bacterium Vibrio Cholerae. Cholera must be present in the population.
