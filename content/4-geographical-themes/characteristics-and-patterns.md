**Settlement site, situation and hierarchy**
The **site** of a settlement refers to the physical characteristics of its location. It is these factors that historically explain the location of a settlement. Today, however, site characteristics include the social, economic and political characteristics of a settlement.

![Picture](assets/siteandsituation.png)

The situation of a settlement explains its location in relation to important physical features, communication routes, and other settlements, thereby locating it within a regional context. \
