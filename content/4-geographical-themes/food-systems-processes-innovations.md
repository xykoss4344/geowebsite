**1. Farms as systems**
Applying the systems approach to subsistence rice farming in the northern Philippines - use [**this magazine double spread**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gMooBo-VRuqBO47NsAXyRkkn_fDPy_vi/view?usp=sharing) for the information, and [**download this activity sheet**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JtTIHBMO4optC7IanaQDqb5DQlvn-lCUbK8Or-FPRT4/edit?usp=sharing) for you to annotate.

**How sustainable are modern farm systems?**
When you read/watch these resources, think about **the energy efficiency ratio**, the added inputs of water, agrochemicals, fuel, land and the added outputs/consequences of pollution. For HL students, this is a revisit. 
Watch [**this five-minute clip of "HOME"**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nYpq2GkE3DnTyLCGvFElKqlaSEEIXg4T/view?usp=sharing) about the impact of modern farm systems. 
Read [**this article**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1P3n195JOUuQ1l5peBg0iv4mLt7UZR7Xwzd85H0Sl1m4/edit?usp=sharing) about agrochemicals and intensive farming.
Watch [**this clip**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ff1_sWd8uLUD4nonXKmZP6pFHUmQ5YiV/view?usp=sharing)of Sir David Attenborough ("Extinction" 2020) on the impact of farming on **biodiversity**. 
If you haven't seen it before, watch [**the hidden cost of hamburgers**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jconD5sb_4_NtejgMkK2SzewDwmyE20-/view?usp=sharing) - its water footprint and the inefficient/unsustainable production of beef.
