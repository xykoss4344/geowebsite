**Focusing on Vung Tau:** Research with a partner and complete this case study of an urban tourism hotspot. 
a) The original factors that led Vung Tau to become a tourist hotspot.
                                       i) Think primary tourist resources. 
                                       ii) Can you find a map from the past and present to compare development of the hotspot? 
b) The more recent factors that continue to make it a tourist hotspot today.
                                       i) Think secondary tourist resources.
                                       ii) What data can you find out about numbers of visitors?
c) Think about how Vung Tau benefits the local community (SEEP).
d) Think about the negative consequences of excessive tourism (SEEP).
