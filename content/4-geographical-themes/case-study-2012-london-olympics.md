**Task 1 - Factors that influence hosting international sporting events**
Complete[**this activity**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GLQDJ0HauvmJxCLccYOmSeOgjzrd7peAiVibOfOXmEU/edit?usp=sharing)to consider what geographic factors (political, economic and cultural) influence the choice of hosts of major international sporting events like The Olympics. Be ready to justify your ideas! 
**Extra** - visit [**this web page**](http://geographical.co.uk/places/mapping/item/1845-cartogram-special-olympics) that displays world maps that indicate participation and success in The Olympics. How does POWER influences the patterns?

**Task 2 - The legacy of London Olympics 2012**
Introducing the London Olympics - [**presentation**](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1c5tmGs_baY73VT1MzgBA0nT_FL1O_Bhu3lcJrUPoHm4/edit?usp=sharing)
