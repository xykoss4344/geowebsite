**Leisure, Sport & Tourism**

As more people join the “global middle class”, they have disposable incomes, allowing participation in tourism, including international travel and different types of sport. Sport can also be an important use of leisure time for people on low incomes who cannot afford to participate in tourism.

While tourism often has an urban focus, rural areas provide another important geographical setting for touristic activities, including walking, enjoying wilderness, engaging in extreme sports or visiting heritage sites. The uses of places vary greatly depending on physical geography, history, and the level of economic development.
