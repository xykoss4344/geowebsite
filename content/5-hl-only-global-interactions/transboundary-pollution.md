**Our case study: "The Haze", Southeast Asia**

**Transboundary pollution** originates in one country but crosses borders via natural pathways (such as prevailing winds), affecting another country's environment, economy, and health. 

For our investigation, we will focus on "the Haze" in Southeast Asia, particularly August to October 2026. 

You will collaborate in small groups to find out its causes, consequences, and responses, using the questions below to guide you. To create a thorough case study, make sure you document specific statistics and places.
