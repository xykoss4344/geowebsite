**London: A "melting pot" of cultural diversity** **Melting Pot—A** cultural process that involves different communities mixing over time to form a more uniform culture which combines traits drawn from the traditions of each of the original communities.
[**A presentation**](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/12TC05px4FEJnufdHRUvTdGOdPgJ38-gBwvyRBi4pyf8/edit?usp=sharing) to introduce London as a case study of cultural diversity.

**"Londonistan" and the 'ghettoization' of neighbourhoods?** **Ghetto: A** part of a city in which members of a minority group make up the majority of residents, typically as a result of social, legal, or economic pressure. The term may have been used originally in Venice to describe the part of the city in which Jews were restricted and segregated.
