For your glossaries...

The role of **civil societies** - "A civil society is composed of all the civic and social organizations or movements that form the basis of a functionary society, and work in the area between the household, the private sector and the state to negotiate matters of public concern. Civic societies include non-governmental organizations (NGOs), community groups, trade unions, academic institutions and faith based organisations".

**International Mindedness** - "A way of thinking that is receptive to ideas from different countries and recognizes that all people belong to a networked international community that is pluralistic, culturally diverse and meritocratic. It also involves an appreciation of the complexity of our world and our interactions with one another".
