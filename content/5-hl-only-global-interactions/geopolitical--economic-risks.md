[New and emerging threats](https://geogjon.weebly.com/new--emerging-threats.html) - tax avoidance, disruptive technological innovations 
[Renewed nationalism/tribalism in response to globalisation](https://geogjon.weebly.com/correlation-between-globalization-and-nationalism.html) - examples of economic and geopolitical tension today
