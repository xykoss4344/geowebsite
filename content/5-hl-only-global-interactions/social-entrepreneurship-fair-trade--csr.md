**Team Task**: \
In your groups, explore the relevant resources to build understanding**,** focusing on either Microfinance, Fair Trade, or Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR). Create a brief presentation for the class (share your **[presentation here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Me-gUGac5prJGllOqJMpaWZucnXuMDgP?usp=sharing)**).
Remember to:
Explain the concept/ idea,
Research & describe an example (how does it create positive social change in communities?) and
Critique the concept.

**Independent comprehension.** 
Outline the benefits of microfinance. 
Examine the critics' view of microfinance.\
Briefly explain the idea of the Fair Trade example you have seen. 
Discuss the most significant critique of Fair Trade.\
Choose a company that operates in Vietnam or your home country. Research its CSR. Outline the potential advantages of the goals and any potential omissions **OR** examine the advantages and disadvantages of CSR in general. 
Why is CSR likely to become a greater issue in Vietnam over the next decade? Outline the advantages and disadvantages of TNCs using Vietnam as a source of cheap labour.

**Extension**
Exam-style extended written question, which asks you to apply your knowledge and understanding of a fairly narrow topic. This time, the command word is "explain" instead of "analyse". 
**"Explain why it might be hard to establish how human development varies between different countries"** (12 marks)
