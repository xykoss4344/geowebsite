**The 'Development Cable' model**

The model illustrates the multidimensional way human development can be measured ...

The core of the cable is the electric current of economic growth, technology and enterprise of development.

The outer layer represents the many different factors that provide the ‘power’ for development cable. They can also be the results of development, too.
