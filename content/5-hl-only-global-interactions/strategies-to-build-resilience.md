**1. How are governments building resilience through TNC reshoring strategies?**

**Reshoring** - Also known as on-shoring or back-shoring, this involves a TNC abandoning length supply chains and instead returning production operations to the country  where it is headquartered. The company will no longer make use of a spatial division of labor.

"What people are waking up to is the interconnectedness of global trade - a single missing chip from Japan can shut down an American factory on the other side of the world." R. Ward (Chief Exec of Lloyd's of London).

Many TNCs have discovered that outsourcing can increase their exposure to different kinds of risks. So how do they respond?

2. How are governments and civil society groups building greater resilience through crowdsourcing technologies?
**Crowdsourcing** -The process of sourcing ideas, services, finances and information from the public via the Internet in order to benefit from the collective abilities of a large group of people.
**Crowdfunding -** Raising sums of money for a good cause or innovation by asking a large number of people to donate a small amount each using an online platform.
